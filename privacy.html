Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At Abdullah Psychotic (AP), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our store or purchase from our exclusive drops.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, interact with our brand, or subscribe to drop alerts, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, shipping address, billing address, email address, and telephone number.
  • Transactional Data: Order details, purchase history, and payment status processed securely through encrypted third-party gateways.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and site interaction cookies to optimize your browsing experience.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is collected solely to provide an unparalleled luxury client experience:

  • Processing, fulfilling, and delivering your orders and custom items.
  • Communicating critical order updates, shipping notifications, and tracking numbers.
  • Providing priority customer support regarding orders or product inquiries.
  • Sending exclusive drop releases, private pre-orders, and brand updates. You may unsubscribe at any time.

Data Security & Encryption

We implement strict technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All payment transactions are processed using industry-standard SSL encryption and secured payment processors. We never store raw credit card numbers on our servers.

Information Sharing & Third Parties

We respect your confidentiality. Abdullah Psychotic does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We share data only with trusted service partners required for fulfilling your purchases, such as payment gateways, logistics/shipping couriers, and essential fraud protection services.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes cookies and similar technologies to enhance performance, remember items in your shopping bag, and analyze web traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the store may function with limited capability.

Your Rights & Data Requests

Depending on your location, you hold rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or request the erasure of your personal data stored with us. To submit a data request, please reach out through our Contact page .

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact our support team through our Contact page .
