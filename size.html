HOW TO MEASURE

CHEST

Measure around the fullest part of your chest while keeping the measuring tape horizontal and comfortable.

SHOULDER

Measure from one shoulder seam to the other across the back.

LENGTH

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the bottom hem.

FOOT LENGTH

Place your foot flat on the floor and measure from the heel to the longest toe.

PLEASE NOTE: Measurements may vary slightly depending on the product design, fabric and manufacturing process. For oversized products, consider the intended relaxed fit before selecting your size.
