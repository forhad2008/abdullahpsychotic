The Beginning
ABDULLAH PSYCHOTIC · 001

Before the brand,
there was an ordinary person.

Hi,My name is Abdullah Forhad. There was nothing extraordinary about me when I started. I was simply an ordinary person who wanted to become something more.

I wasn’t born with some rare talent that made everyone notice me. I didn’t possess a special ability, an unusual habit, or anything extraordinary that immediately set me apart from the people around me. Nor was my life shaped by some great tragedy—a devastating loss, the death of someone I loved, or an event so painful that it left me broken, angry, and fundamentally different from everyone else. I had no dramatic origin story. No extraordinary gift. No tragedy that suddenly transformed me into someone special. My life, at least from the outside, was ordinary. I was simply another person among millions—nothing particularly remarkable, nothing that demanded attention. And yet, somewhere along the way, I began to change. Not because the world had chosen me. Not because I was born different. But because of what I slowly became.

I was ordinary.

And for a long time, I thought that meant I had to remain ordinary. But somewhere inside me, there was always a question:

Why should I become just another person in the crowd?

I didn't want to spend my life being defined by what I was naturally given. I wanted to build something that belonged to me — something that could not be explained by talent, luck, appearance, or circumstances.

So I started looking at myself differently.

I realized that perhaps being different isn't always about possessing something rare. Sometimes, being rare is a decision.

I began to understand that I didn't need to be the strongest person in the room. I didn't need to be the most naturally gifted. I didn't need to have everything figured out from the beginning.

What I needed was the willingness to continue.

To work when the excitement disappeared. To endure when progress became invisible. To learn when I felt behind. To begin again when something failed.

I don't believe strength is what makes someone extraordinary. I believe endurance does.

I don't believe talent is enough. I believe hard work gives talent its meaning.

And I don't believe you have to be born special to become someone different.

You can build yourself.

That belief became the foundation of Abdullah Psychotic.

This brand isn't a celebration of perfection. It is a reflection of becoming.

Every piece represents the idea that identity is not something you simply discover. It is something you build.

Slowly. Through discipline. Through failure. Through patience. Through countless decisions that nobody else sees.

I started as an ordinary person. Maybe I still am.

But I decided that being ordinary at the beginning doesn't mean being ordinary forever.

I don't want to be separated from the world because I was born different.

I want to be separated because I chose to become different.

And that choice is where everything began.

01

THE ORDINARY

I started without a remarkable advantage. No extraordinary talent. No perfect confidence. No special formula.

Just an ordinary person with an unusual desire: I wanted to become more than what I was given.

02

THE BELIEF

I stopped waiting for strength to appear. I stopped believing that talent was the only path to greatness.

Instead, I chose something much simpler: work, endurance, and repetition.

You don't always need to be the best to keep moving. Sometimes you only need to be the person who refuses to stop.

03

THE BECOMING

Abdullah Psychotic became an expression of that decision. Not about being born different. Not about pretending to be perfect.

It is about building an identity strong enough to stand on its own.

You don't have to discover something extraordinary inside yourself. Sometimes, you have to build it.
