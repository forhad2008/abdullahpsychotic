The Beginning · ABDULLAH PSYCHOTIC · 001

Before The Brand, There Was An Ordinary Person.

Hi, My name is Abdullah Forhad. There was nothing extraordinary about me when I started. I was simply an ordinary person who wanted to become something more.

I wasn’t born with some rare talent that made everyone notice me. I didn’t possess a special ability, an unusual habit, or anything extraordinary that immediately set me apart from the people around me.

Nor was my life shaped by some great tragedy—a devastating loss, the death of someone I loved, or an event so painful that it left me broken, angry, and fundamentally different from everyone else. I had no dramatic origin story. No extraordinary gift. No tragedy that suddenly transformed me into someone special.

My life, at least from the outside, was ordinary. I was simply another person among millions—nothing particularly remarkable, nothing that demanded attention.

And yet, somewhere along the way, I began to change. Not because the world had chosen me. Not because I was born different. But because of what I slowly became.

DOC.002 // THE TURNING POINT
CONVICTION · 100%
Chapter II · The Turning Point

Being Rare Is A Decision.

I was ordinary. And for a long time, I thought that meant I had to remain ordinary. But somewhere inside me, there was always a question: Why should I become just another person in the crowd?

I didn't want to spend my life being defined by what I was naturally given. I wanted to build something that belonged to me — something that could not be explained by talent, luck, appearance, or circumstances.

So I started looking at myself differently. I realized that perhaps being different isn't always about possessing something rare. Sometimes, being rare is a decision.

I began to understand that I didn't need to be the strongest person in the room. I didn't need to be the most naturally gifted. I didn't need to have everything figured out from the beginning.

What I needed was the willingness to continue:

To work when the excitement disappeared.
To endure when progress became invisible.
To learn when I felt behind.
To begin again when something failed.

I don't believe strength is what makes someone extraordinary. I believe endurance does.

I don't believe talent is enough. I believe hard work gives talent its meaning.

And I don't believe you have to be born special to become someone different. You can build yourself.

That belief became the foundation of Abdullah Psychotic.
